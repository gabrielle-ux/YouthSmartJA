Platform Features

YouthSmartJA is an AI-powered job matching and career guidance dashboard that helps students move from resume upload to skill growth.

📄

Resume Upload

Students upload a resume before accessing personalized matches.

🧠

NLP Processing

The system extracts keywords, skills, and useful resume signals.

🎯

Cosine Matching

Jobs are scored based on similarity between resume text and job requirements.

⚙️

Preference Scoring

Job type, location, and user preferences affect the final job feed.

🛣️

Guidance Mode

Dijkstra-style pathing identifies the best missing skills to learn next.

🔖

Bookmarks

Students can save jobs and return to them while tracking progress.
